Common questions

Why is there a paywall?

Full access requires a subscription or lifetime purchase. There is no free content tier after download. Prices are shown in the App by the App Store or Google Play (marketing references use USD: $4.99/month, $14.99/year, $24.99 lifetime).

How do I restore purchases?

On the paywall or in Settings, tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple Account (iPhone/iPad) or Google account (Android) used to buy.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Rail Track Safety Prep → Cancel. Or use Manage Subscriptions.

On Android: open the Google Play app → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Rail Track Safety Prep → Cancel. Or use Google Play subscriptions.

Is this official Network Rail / Sentinel training?

No. This is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with Network Rail, RSSB, or the Sentinel scheme.
